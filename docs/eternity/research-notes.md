## Eternity Finance today: main gaps vs Tally
No credit/debit notes, contra, TDS/TCS, reverse charge, HSN/SAC, e-invoice/e-way bill, GSTR formats, round-off (account exists, unused), discounts, tax-inclusive rates; no bank import/reconciliation, payment links, cheque printing; no cost centres, budgets, inventory; no day book, cash flow, ageing, ratios, comparatives; no exports or print layout (no amount in words, HSN, place of supply, signature); no recurring entries, reminders, email/WhatsApp; no audit log for masters; default accounts not editable; receipts without invoice become advances and cannot be matched later; CampusTwin fees not posted to Finance; students/parents not auto-created as parties.

## Eternity Alumni today: main gaps vs AlmaShines
No email/SMS/WhatsApp at all (approvals, invitations, reminders, newsletters, birthdays); no automatic alumni from CampusTwin graduates; no online giving (manual recording only), no PDF/emailed 80G receipts, no Form 10BD/10BE; events cannot be edited, no end time, reminders, check-in, tickets, waitlist, RSVP export; jobs without approval or editing; news without images/comments; no mentorship, groups/chapters, membership, ID card, map, photo, DOB; import by paste only (no file), no LinkedIn/higher studies import; email-only de-duplication, no merge; directory privacy is a single switch and contact columns are readable through the API (fix); declined applicants cannot re-apply; join code cannot be regenerated; no alumni record export.
